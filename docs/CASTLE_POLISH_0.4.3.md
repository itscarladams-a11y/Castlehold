# Castlehold 0.4.3 — Castle polish

This small art pass improves the fortress without changing Castlehold's gameplay layout or destructible gate behavior.

## Visual changes
- Added machicolation-style stone supports below the parapets of the side walls, gatehouse and keep.
- Strengthened the gatehouse crown with a projecting overhang and additional slit detail.
- Improved the keep frontage with alternating corner quoins, shallow buttresses, a roof dormer and two chimneys over the adjoining hall.
- Added extra crown detail and a brazier to the mage tower.

## Scope
These changes are purely environmental art and leave battle speed, audio, wave balance, checkpoints and structure HP unchanged. The gate leaves and portcullis remain independent damage pieces.
