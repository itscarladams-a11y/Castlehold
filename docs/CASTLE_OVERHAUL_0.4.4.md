# Castlehold 0.4.4 — Castle overhaul

This pass is meant to be visibly stronger than the earlier polish pass. It focuses on a more dramatic in-game silhouette while preserving gameplay layout and destructible gate behavior.

## Visual changes
- Enlarged the gatehouse flanking towers and added a raised central watch chamber with its own roof and banner.
- Added heavier front buttresses and a chunkier gatehouse crown.
- Increased the keep massing and height, broadened the roof, and expanded the frontal detail bands.
- Added twin roof turrets, extra banners, a taller keep watchtower and stronger hall proportions.
- Upgraded the mage tower and roofed the side corner towers for a more heroic skyline.

## Scope
These changes are environmental art only. Battle speed, audio behavior, unit balance, economy, checkpoints, structure HP and gate destruction logic remain unchanged from 0.4.3.
