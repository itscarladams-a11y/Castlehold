# Castlehold 0.4.5 — Bosses every 20 waves

The active 100-wave siege now places one boss encounter at waves **20, 40, 60, 80 and 100**.

- 20: The Gatebreaker
- 40: Dreadscale Rider
- 60: The Ashcaller
- 80: Stone-Eye Warlord
- 100: The Ironjaw King

Stone-Eye Warlord fills the added fifth milestone. It reuses Castlehold's authored Gatebreaker cyclops model/animation rig but has its own UnitData/BossData profile: 11,800 HP, 25% armor, stronger melee/splash, a 9-second Earthshatter special and a 2,250-gold bounty. Ironjaw remains the final boss and keeps its two reinforcement roars.

Version-two checkpoints remain compatible. A checkpoint already past a newly moved boss milestone does not retroactively spawn that boss.
