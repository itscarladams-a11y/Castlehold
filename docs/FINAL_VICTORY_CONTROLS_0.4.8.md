# Castlehold 0.4.8 — Final Victory / 4× / Save for Later

## Final boss contract
Wave 120 is one complete final encounter. The Gravecaller may die before his raised or accompanying attackers. His death changes the HUD to `FINAL BOSS • GRAVECALLER'S LEGION`, but the campaign remains in the final phase until pending reinforcements are empty and no live enemies remain. Only then does Castlehold display `VICTORY`, stop combat and play the dedicated triumphant fanfare.

## Battle controls
The speed ladder is now 1× / 2× / 3× / 4×. Speed can be changed during combat or while paused and persists in version-two saves. Pause continues to freeze wave timers, movement, projectiles and skeletal animation.

## Save for later
The top-bar Save button first pauses the battle and then writes the current campaign state. It preserves troops, enemies, HP, positions, economy, castle condition, repairs, pending reinforcements, boss ritual state, raised skeleton state, wave timing and selected speed. The in-session Retry Wave checkpoint is intentionally not replaced by a manual Save. Reopening the game restores the most recently written state paused. In-flight projectiles and cosmetic debris remain transient. Android backgrounding also attempts a current-state save.

## Victory audio
`assets/audio/victory_fanfare.wav` is an original 5.2-second synthesized fanfare generated by `tools/build_victory_fanfare.py`. It plays on a dedicated effects player so a saturated combat-effects pool cannot suppress the victory cue.
